Straight lines are loops here

This table is a sphere. A spin-free shot travels along a great circle — the sphere's version of a straight line. It never stops turning, and if it has enough power it comes all the way around and hits you from behind.

The pocket is not on the near face. Getting the black ball there means sending it the long way around.

  • AimDrag from the cue ball toward where you want it to go. Drag length = power.
  • LookDrag anywhere else to orbit the sphere and see the far side.
  • HintThe dashed ghost line is the full infinite geodesic. The solid line is how far your shot actually reaches.
